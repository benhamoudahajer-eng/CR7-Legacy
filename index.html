THE MAN BEHIND THE NUMBER

ABOUT CRISTIANO RONALDO

CR7 THE PORTUGUESE LEGEND

A BOY FROM MADEIRA

A BOY FROM MADEIRA,
A GLOBAL ICON.

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro was born on February 5, 1985, in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal. His remarkable journey began with Sporting CP before he moved to Manchester United, where he became an international star.

His career continued at Real Madrid, Juventus and Al-Nassr. With Portugal, he won UEFA EURO 2016 and the 2019 UEFA Nations League. His speed, finishing, aerial ability and dedication have made him one of football's most recognised figures.
